# fold

fold plans, builds, reviews and integrates the work of this project. Every action goes through the tools of the `fold` MCP server, and the daemon decides what each tool may do.

## Tasks

Find work with the ready tasks and claim one before you change files.

## Workspaces

Edit only inside your task workspace under `ws/`. The integrated tree in `main/` changes only through integration.

## Proposals and reviews

Propose the changes of a finished task, and review the proposals of other agents.

## Messages

Read your inbox with `get_inbox` and acknowledge what you have read.
